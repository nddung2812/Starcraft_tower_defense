Tower defense where you lead the fight

BREACHLINE: 29 is a sci-fi tower defense and real-time strategy game. Your towers are soldiers, walkers, tanks and gunships, and they go where you send them. Wall off a canyon, garrison a bunker, then march out and burn the hives before the next wave lands.

StarCraft-style command

If you grew up on classic RTS games like StarCraft, your hands already know what to do. Box-select a squad, right-click to give orders, bind control groups to number keys and watch the minimap for the next attack.

A Starship Troopers last stand

The mood comes from the doomed outposts of Starship Troopers: a thin line of soldiers, a narrow canyon and more bugs than bullets. As the rounds climb, the swarm attacks from up to four sides.

RPG-style progression

Promote soldiers through the ranks, unlock armory upgrades with every kill and take silver and gold treasure from fallen bosses. Each boss also lets you choose an evolution that changes your army for the rest of the run.

Pick your fight

Play for five minutes or settle in for a long defense. Every mode uses the same army, the same swarm and a battlefield generated from a seed.

Endless mode

Start with three soldiers and hold for as long as you can. Your score is every bug you kill before the outpost falls.

Survival mode

A match you can win. Choose from one to seven hives, set how fierce the swarm is, then destroy every hive before your base falls.

Defense mode

The battle waits while you plan. Lay out your walls, bunkers and guns at your own pace, then resume when the line is ready.

Daily challenge

One new map every day. Chase your best score, build a day streak and send a friend a link to the same battlefield.

Questions from new recruits

Is BREACHLINE: 29 free to play?

Yes. The game is free and runs in your browser. You do not need a download or an account.

Is this an official StarCraft or Starship Troopers game?

No. BREACHLINE: 29 is an independent game with its own world, units and story. Its RTS controls and last-stand mood are inspired by those classics, but it is not connected to their owners.

Is BREACHLINE: 29 an RPG?

It is a tower defense and real-time strategy hybrid with RPG-style progression. You promote units through ranks, earn armory upgrades from kills, collect boss treasure and choose evolutions that last for the whole run.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Tap a unit, then tap the ground or an enemy to give an order. Pinch to zoom and twist to rotate the camera. A mouse and keyboard give you the full set of RTS shortcuts.

Is there multiplayer?

The battle is single-player, but you can compete with friends. After a run you can send a challenge link that opens the same map, and the daily challenge gives everyone the same battlefield.

Does the game save my progress?

Yes. Your run is saved in your browser at the start of each round, so you can continue later on the same device. Achievements and cosmetic rewards carry across runs.
